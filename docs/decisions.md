# Decisions

- **2026-07-27 — Private/public boundary:** Career CSVs and drafts remain outside `src/` and `public/`; production code contains only public article metadata.
- **2026-07-27 — Content model:** Use a small JavaScript article manifest rather than adding a CMS or Markdown dependency. Add public HTML pages only when a draft is approved.
- **2026-07-27 — First draft:** Use Epistack because repository evidence is extensive and no thesis confidentiality is implicated. Keep it draft-only.
- **2026-07-27 — Tracking:** Use portable CSV plus standard-library Python scripts; no database, authentication, or SaaS.
- **2026-07-28 — CV generation:** Move the CV off Canva into `cv/`, built locally from LaTeX with `cv/build.ps1`. Source is version-controlled and the PDF is reproducible; Canva was an unversioned external dependency. Awesome-CV is vendored in `cv/awesome-cv/` so builds never need the network.
- **2026-07-28 — Two local patches to the vendored Awesome-CV class:** Source Sans 3 replaced with Lato (MiKTeX does not package the former), and `\RequirePackage{bookmark}` disabled (MiKTeX's bookmark 1.31 rejects the `hluatex` driver chosen by hyperref 7.01o; it only suppressed a rerun warning, which the two-pass build already handles). Redo both if the class is ever re-pulled from upstream.
- **2026-07-28 — Career-support functions split by what can be systematised:** Assessment, materials, positioning, cadence and record-keeping are handled in-repo. Market intelligence (`market-intelligence.md`) and interview rehearsal (`interview-prep.md`) are now covered too. Network and morale cannot be self-supplied by any tool and are deliberately not modelled — the outreach drafts exist to lower the cost of the human step, not to replace it.
- **2026-07-28 — One career overview, generated not maintained:** `scripts/generate_career_overview.py` emits `career/overview.md` and `career/overview.html` from the existing documents. Nothing is hand-kept, so it cannot drift. It supersedes `generate_career_summary.py`, which reported only the application pipeline — the least populated part of the picture while not applying. Both outputs are ignored, like `summary.md`.
- **2026-07-28 — CV publishing is a separate step:** `build.ps1` writes to `cv/` only. `-Publish` copies into `public/` and `docs/`. The live CV is never overwritten as a side effect of a build.
- **2026-08-25 — AI 2040 reader is a static sub-route:** Keep the scroll-led AI 2040 companion in `public/ai2040/` so `/ai2040/` works as a direct GitHub Pages URL without adding a router to the portfolio. It is explicitly an attributed, condensed reader rather than a verbatim mirror of the AI Futures Project's publication.
- **2026-08-25 — AI 2040 dashboard animation stays framework-free:** Recreate the reference's scroll-linked visual language with high-resolution Canvas 2D, locally served Natural Earth 1:110m country geometry, and DOM fallbacks. This keeps the direct static route self-contained while supporting globe rotation, map markers, trajectory branching, live counters, treemap morphing, reduced motion, and responsive resizing.
- **2026-08-25 — The Netherlands scenario is an independent sibling route:** Preserve `/ai2040/` unchanged and develop `/ai2040netherlands/` as a static fork. Its primary visual argument is a permanently visible five-series dashboard: compute, Dutch control, supply-chain leverage, service leverage and oversight coverage. A combined chart shades the widening relative gap between compute and the composite Dutch response. Provenance remains part of the interface: starting estimates are ranged, scenario paths are dashed, and missing public measurements remain visible rather than being presented as facts.
- **2026-08-25 — Dutch timelines branch at a real choice boundary:** Before the 2030 choice, keep the reached moment on the far-right edge and reveal only the common 2026–2030 history. Remove all later chapters and the footer from layout so the reader physically cannot scroll beyond the choice. Selecting Regie, Markt or Uitstel restores the later page and animates that path’s 2034–2040 data into the second half of the charts. Centre the fixed AI2040 geometry on wider laptop viewports while retaining its 700 px prose column, 455 px HUD, 28.5 px internal inset and 51.3 px centre whitespace.
- **2026-08-25 — Provenance is a dashboard view, not a disclaimer:** The Bronnen tab states that none of the five exact dashboard bands has a public national time series, then separates each scenario value from its official anchor or proxy (EZ GPU inventory, Rekenkamer cloud dependence, ASML EUV/service data and AP staffing). Date negative findings as a source audit and never render an absent measurement as zero.
- **2026-09-01 — Unpreparedness becomes the article’s main claim:** Replace the generic optionality narrative with “Wij zijn niet goed voorbereid.” Map its diagnosis, international-position argument, fiscal exposure, no-regret measures and conclusion onto the existing 2026–2040 scroll anchors without showing years in the article headings. Keep the 2030 choice boundary and dashboard paths as the interactive counterfactual layer. Where supplied prose conflicts with the source audit, prefer the evidenced formulation (no reliable national compute count rather than no estimate; latest official defence-pension transfer estimate).
- **2026-09-01 — The published draft is linear and the dashboard is an official baseline:** Remove the 2030 choice gate, all policy branches and every invented scenario value. Preserve the supplied article verbatim as one continuous reading path. Drive the five permanent widgets with CEP 2026 series for GDP growth, labour force, hours worked, labour productivity and the labour-income share; reveal the common CPB projection on scroll and show the absent AI variant as a data gap rather than estimating it.
- **2026-11-05 — Long-form write-ups are static sub-routes with one shared stylesheet:** `/warming-control/` (internship) and `/vessel-targeting/` (thesis) live in `public/` like the other static routes, so no router is needed. Both link `public/writeup.css`, which copies the homepage's light-only look (gray-50 page, white bordered cards, system sans). The homepage gets an Experience section for the internship; the thesis goes first under Research.
- **2026-11-05 — Internship write-up follows the publication position of 2026-07-28:** The page covers method, the MPC-versus-PID comparison, the measured control performance (σ ≈ 0.03 °C outlet stability, the report value, consistent with the raw April log; the presentation's 0.30 °C appears to be a typo) and personal learning. It leaves out the hand-sensor anaesthesia-detection result (unreleased product recommendation), the costed BOM, the business case including the energy-saving figures, board-level firmware detail, internal documents and colleague names. The water-bag protocol limitation is stated as permanent, and the grade (8/10) is shown. The bench photo was kept on the owner's instruction. British English throughout, per `AGENTS.md`.
- **2026-11-05 — Thesis write-up published at full detail on the owner's instruction:** The owner chose the full write-up with digital-twin results over a topic-only card. Simulated results are labelled as simulated, the physical comparison is stated as in progress, and the conference paper is mentioned without its title.
